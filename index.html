Ten useful phrases from your latest lesson · Updated 18 October 2026

  1. Havâ târik shod.هوا تاریک شد.It got dark.
  2. Dâshtan miraftan sinemâ.داشتن می‌رفتن سینما.They were going to the cinema.
  3. Az didan-e un khoshhâl shodim.از دیدن اون خوشحال شدیم.We became happy from seeing him/her.
  4. Shomâ khoshhâl shodid.شما خوشحال شدید.You became happy. (plural/formal)
  5. Barâdaresho didim.برادرش رو دیدیم.We saw his/her brother.
  6. Raftim sinemâ tâ film bebinim.رفتیم سینما تا فیلم ببینیم.We went to the cinema to watch a film.
  7. Film tamum shod.فیلم تموم شد.The film ended.
  8. Film tamum shode.فیلم تموم شده.The film has finished.
  9. Hamegi bâ ham bargashtim.همگی با هم برگشتیم.We all returned together.
  10. Raftim hamun sinemâ.رفتیم همون سینما.We went to that same cinema.
