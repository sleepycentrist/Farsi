Numbers

Persian uses its own digit shapes. The value and order are the same as English digits, even though Persian writing runs from right to left.

How to build a larger number

Say the largest part first. Join each non-zero part with و, pronounced o in these combinations.

350 · ۳۵۰ 300 + 50 سیصد و پنجاه sisad-o-panjâh
  • 21 · ۲۱: بیست و یک — bist-o-yek
  • 53 · ۵۳: پنجاه و سه — panjâh-o-se
  • 412 · ۴۱۲: چهارصد و دوازده — chahârsad-o-davâzdah
  • 654 · ۶۵۴: ششصد و پنجاه و چهار — sheshsad-o-panjâh-o-chahâr
  • 1,234 · ۱٬۲۳۴: هزار و دویست و سی و چهار — hezâr-o-dovist-o-si-o-chahâr

Useful larger units: 1,000 = هزار (hezâr) and 1,000,000 = یک میلیون (yek milyoon).

Persian alphabet

Persian has 32 letters and is written from right to left. Most letters join to neighbouring letters, so their shape can change inside a word. Short vowels are usually understood rather than written.

Three useful reading rules

  1. Read Persian words from right to left.
  2. Several letters can represent the same modern Persian sound, including the different s, z and t letters.
  3. The letters ا، د، ذ، ر، ز، ژ and و do not join to the letter that follows them.
